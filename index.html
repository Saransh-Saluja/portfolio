How I work

The same loop on every project, from first question to shipped screen.

Understand

Who is this for, what are they trying to do, what does the business need.

Sketch

Cheap wireframes to compare flows before anything gets polished.

Prototype

High-fidelity screens, then clickable flows to test the real path.

Test

Watch people use it, note where they hesitate, change the design.

Ship

Build it in code and keep the system consistent across screens.

Design-system thinking

Because I also write the front end, I design for how things get built.

Tokens first

Colour, spacing and radius are defined once and reused. The playground at the top of this page is the idea in miniature.

Performance is UX

I've used lazy loading, memoization and code splitting to keep large financial reports fast to open and scroll.

Trust by design

Audit logs, role-based access and secure sign-in shape what users see. I treat them as part of the experience, not backend extras.
